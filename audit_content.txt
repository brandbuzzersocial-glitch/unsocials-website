01 - Executive Summary
This audit covers every page of the Unsocials Thailand website across five dimensions: content accuracy, messaging consistency, design quality, technical functionality, and conversion optimisation. The site has strong bones - the concept, brand voice, and case study content are genuinely compelling. However, there are critical inconsistencies that undermine credibility and several broken or placeholder elements that reduce trust.

The biggest risk: a potential client in Thailand who lands on this site will find contradictions (Bangkok vs Pattaya), missing social links, placeholder widgets, and inconsistent numbers. These are fixable in under a week and should be treated as urgent.

04 - Design Recommendations

DESIGN FIXES AND IMPROVEMENTS

4.1 - Navigation Issues
Homepage navbar appears TWICE in the HTML - code duplication bug. Remove one instance.
Services and About pages are missing &apos;Home&apos; in the nav - add it for consistent navigation across all pages.
The EN/TH language toggle exists but Thai version does not appear to be live. Either build Thai version or remove toggle until ready - a non-functional toggle looks broken.
&apos;Book a Call&apos; CTA goes to WhatsApp on homepage but to contact.html on Services page. Standardise to WhatsApp everywhere - it converts better.

4.2 - Hero Section
Reel videos (r1 to r4) in the hero are excellent. Verify they autoplay on mobile - many browsers block mobile autoplay. Add a static poster image fallback.
&apos;INITIALISING&apos; loading screen is a cool effect but if it takes over 1.5 seconds it frustrates mobile users. Reduce animation duration or add a progress bar.
&apos;Scroll&apos; indicator at the bottom of the hero is good UX. Make it a bouncing animated arrow to draw more attention.

4.3 - Testimonials Section
The yellow star rating (5 stars) is good. But the profile images (AI characters) undercut authenticity completely.
Replace Ava and Alex profile images with: real client logos, hotel exterior photos, or simple monogram initials on a coloured circle background.
The Xperience Stays review has no actual result mentioned - just sentiment. Add a number. Any number.

4.4 - Mobile Experience
Ticker/marquee animations need testing on iOS Safari - these commonly break on Apple devices.
Contact form has no visible validation states. Add real-time validation and a clear success message so users know their submission worked.
Google Maps embed uses generic Pattaya coordinates - update to your exact office pin location.
The case study cards on work.html have an arrow (up-right) implying clickthrough to detail. Since nothing happens, either make them work or remove the arrow.

08 - Final Verdict

What Is Working Well
The brand voice is strong. We Make It Inevitable is a great tagline. Case studies are real and impressive. The AI creative angle with Ava and Alex is a genuine differentiator - no agency in Thailand is doing this. Visual design is premium and appropriate for the market.
The Risk Right Now
The site undermines its own credibility with inconsistent data, broken links, wrong city names, and placeholder content. A hotel GM in Pattaya doing a 10-minute check before calling will find these issues and lose confidence. Entirely fixable - treat as highest priority this week.
The Opportunity Ahead
With Week 1 fixes alone, this site goes from startup to serious agency. Month 1 additions including blog, individual case study pages, pricing, and team section will make it a lead-generating machine. The bones are excellent. Execution just needs to catch up to the ambition.
