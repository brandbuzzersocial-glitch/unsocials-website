The Story

The Challenge

Alexa Beach Club needed to scale table reservations and event attendance in Pattaya's competitive nightlife scene. Traditional marketing wasn't cutting it — they needed qualified leads that converted automatically.

Our Approach

Full-funnel strategy across Meta and Google with WhatsApp click-to-chat as the primary conversion. Automated flows captured intent, answered FAQs, and confirmed reservations — zero human intervention needed.

The Result

3.2x ROAS achieved within 45 days. Table reservations scaled dramatically with WhatsApp automation handling the majority of bookings. The system runs and optimises on autopilot.
