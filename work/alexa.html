The Story

The Challenge

Pattaya is known for nightlife, but few tourists knew where to go by day. Alexa Beach Club had strong potential but almost no social visibility — Instagram reach was stuck at 26K, Facebook at 297K, and direct reservation inquiries were minimal.

Our Approach

Reels showcased high-energy day parties, pool sessions, and birthday packages, while positioning the private beach as an intimate destination-wedding venue. Meta ads drove that content further, engineered to push traffic and direct messages straight into the booking flow via WhatsApp and Messenger.

The Result

Instagram reach grew from 26K to 2.6M, Facebook reach from 297K to 711K — with 200+ direct reservation messages and 20,000+ link clicks flowing straight to their profile. Real, verifiable business growth, not just impressions — and Alexa is now known as Pattaya's daytime destination, not just its nightlife.
