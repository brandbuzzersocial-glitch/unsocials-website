The Story

The Challenge

Elysium was bleeding revenue through OTA commissions — Agoda, Booking.com, and Expedia taking 15-25% per reservation. Direct bookings were nearly non-existent with zero performance marketing infrastructure.

Our Approach

We built a complete direct booking engine from scratch. Google Hotel Ads captured high-intent travellers, Meta targeted lookalike audiences, and WhatsApp click-to-chat funnels replaced landing pages — converting at 3x the industry rate.

The Result

Within 60 days, direct bookings surged 286%. OTA commissions dropped 62%. The property hit a sustained 3.4x ROAS, turning ad spend into a profitable revenue engine.
