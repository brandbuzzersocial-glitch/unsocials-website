The Challenge

Elysium Pattaya was bleeding revenue through OTA commissions — Agoda, Booking.com, and Expedia were taking 15-25% of every reservation. Direct bookings were almost non-existent, and the property had no performance marketing infrastructure.

Our Approach

We built a complete direct booking engine from scratch. Google Hotel Ads captured high-intent travellers searching for Pattaya accommodation. Meta campaigns targeted lookalike audiences based on past guest data. WhatsApp click-to-chat funnels replaced the traditional landing page, converting at 3x the industry rate.

Every baht of ad spend was tracked end-to-end — from click to check-in. We eliminated guesswork and built a system that scales.

The Result

Within 60 days, direct bookings surged by 286%. OTA commissions dropped by 62%. The property achieved a sustained 3.4x ROAS across all channels, turning their marketing spend into a profitable revenue engine.
