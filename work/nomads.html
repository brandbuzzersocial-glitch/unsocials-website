The Challenge

Nomads Hostel operates three properties across Asia. Each location had inconsistent marketing, no unified brand presence, and was heavily reliant on OTA platforms for bookings. Revenue growth had plateaued.

Our Approach

We implemented a unified performance marketing strategy across all three properties. UGC content from real guests became the foundation of all ad creative — authentic, relatable, and high-converting. Direct booking funnels were built for each property with WhatsApp automation handling enquiries and confirmations.

Social media was transformed from a sporadic afterthought into a systematic growth engine with consistent posting, community engagement, and strategic content calendars.

The Result

Revenue grew 158% across all properties. The direct booking funnel now operates on autopilot, with UGC-powered ads consistently outperforming traditional creative. Each property has its own optimised campaign structure while sharing learnings across the portfolio.
